TIMEX VT102 TERMINAL

A VT102 terminal for Timex 2048/2068-class machines, in 80×24 monochrome text on Timex hi-res 512×192 video, with the 6×8 font from the Timex Terminal TT3000 ROM. This page always runs the latest TAP built from master on an emulated Timex TC2048. Press the ▶ button on the display to start.

There is no host at the other end here, so the terminal runs in loopback: what you type goes through the keyboard mapping, out to the connection layer, back in, and through the VT parser onto the screen. That exercises the real path — it is a working terminal talking to itself. Attaching it to a shell over a WebSocket is issue 1.

ENTER — CR   CAPS+0 — backspace   SYMBOL+SPACE — ESC   CAPS+SYMBOL+letter — Ctrl-letter

Timex video needs our fork of JSSpeccy 3, which adds the TC2048 and TC2068; stock JSSpeccy has no Timex video modes. TAPs are also in the releases.

Emulation by JSSpeccy 3 (GPL-3), in the dtz-labs fork with Timex support.
